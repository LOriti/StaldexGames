# Artwork prompts

Mode: built-in image generation. Four independent generated assets. Runtime

## Earth

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 2D game planet sprite, square 1024 x 1024, transparent background.
Primary request: A beautifully detailed, physically plausible Earth globe for INVASION, a serious mobile planetary-preparedness strategy game. One sphere ONLY, exactly centered; globe disc diameter precisely 84% of image width, margins 8% on all sides. Asia, Indian Ocean and Australia facing the camera, recognisable real geography. North is up. Deep midnight blue ocean, muted slate teal land, fine white cloud systems, subtle warm amber city lights on the night edge. Sunlight from upper left, right limb mostly dark but visible. Fine thin icy blue atmosphere immediately at limb, no broad halo. Photorealistic satellite surface with tasteful cinematic colour grading, restrained contrast, high detail that reads at 80–300 pixels. Perfect circular silhouette, orthographic view, no perspective flattening.
Constraints: actual transparent background outside globe, no stars, no ships, no surrounding objects, no orbit lines, no rings, no labels, no text, no interface, no watermark. The globe is the only asset. This is a usable cutout sprite, not a poster or a mockup.

No aliens, no alien ships, no revealed enemy, no characters in closeup, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no interface or frame.
