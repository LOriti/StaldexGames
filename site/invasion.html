INVASION

A hostile signal has been confirmed. Arrival: 100 years. You are Director of Planetary Preparedness. Most directors only decide how long humanity lasts. A rare few drive them off.

Population is your workforce. Workforce × Industry produces Effort, the base currency.
Knowledge produces Research. Economy and People programs give a small boost now and a permanent boost every year after.
Projects are built and expanded with Effort (more size, more crew and upkeep) and improved with Research (better tech, cheaper to run). They grow 1.5% stronger every year after they're built, so early builds count most. They need crew from Population and Effort upkeep. Understaffed or unfunded capabilities run weaker.
The Empire tab shows how long you'd last and what that rests on. Events let you choose where the hit lands, even into Effort debt. During the invasion you make three war council calls.
