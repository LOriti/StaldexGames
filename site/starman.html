The Territory

What will the sky tell you?

A star chart reading isn't fortune-telling — it's exploration. You bring the questions you've been circling; the chart gives us somewhere real to stand while we look at them.

Self

  • Who am I underneath the version everyone else gets?
  • Why do I keep repeating the same patterns?
  • What are my real strengths — and my blind spots?

Love

  • What do I actually need in a partner — versus what I keep choosing?
  • Are our charts speaking the same language?
  • Why do my relationships end the same way?

Purpose

  • What am I here to do — really?
  • Am I on my path, or just on a path?
  • What creative fire am I sitting on?

Timing

  • Why does everything feel so hard right now?
  • Is this the moment to make the leap?
  • What is the coming year asking of me?

If you saw your own question up there — the sky already has an opinion about it.

The Starman

About Travis

Part shaman, part astronomer of the inner life, Travis has spent years reading charts, holding space, and helping people hear what they already half-knew. His approach is warm, direct, and refreshingly unprecious — the cosmos is vast, but the conversation is human.

No doom, no fortune-telling theatre. Just an honest map of the sky you were born under, and good company while you explore it.
