SEND IT — gameplay outline

The full shape of the game on one page: the loop, the dials you're juggling, the bar, the crawl, kick-ons, the secret bowlo run, and every way the night can end. Design reference — not the game itself.

01 The premise

You're on a Northbridge (Perth) bucks night with Bucko and seven mates. The goal isn't to get as maggot as possible — it's to keep the whole crew, and the whole night, humming for as long as you can. Great nights are managed, not survived.

One night runs 6:00pm → 6:00am (144 ticks; a tick is 5 minutes of game-clock out on the town and 20 minutes once you’re at kick-ons, so a house-party “day” is a real 24 hours while the per-tick feel stays the same). Survive to sunrise and you're offered kick-ons, which can push the session across multiple days. Take one strange fork early and the game quietly becomes something else entirely.

02 The core loop — THE ZONE

Everything orbits your buzz (0–100). Too sober and the party fades; too maggot and you get sloppy, hot, and seated. The sweet spot is THE ZONE: 42–75 — staying in it earns legend faster and keeps the boys and the night alive.

Buzz decays every tick on its own (~2.2/tick out on the town, ~2.8/tick at kick-ons), so you're constantly topping up. Drink to climb, water to come down, food to slow the slide. Drift too far from the zone in either direction and your out-of-control factor rises — which makes bad events more likely and, at the bowlo, makes the night harder to hold.

03 The dials you're juggling

Six things move under the hood. Read them together — a great run keeps buzz in the zone, heat low, boys up, and the card... a problem for tomorrow.

🎯Buzz

Your intoxication, 0–100. Keep it in the zone (42–75). Drink up, water down, food slows the decay.

🚨Heat / notoriety

Police & bouncer attention. Rises with chaos, drugs, and getting caught; too much heat gets you knocked back at doors or ends the night in the cells.

💳The card bill

Every drink and shout stacks up. Doesn't end the night — it's the receipt of shame at the end. Your bank starts sending worried texts.

⭐Legend

Your score. Earned by being in the zone, big moments, shoeys, saving the night. This is what the end-of-night rank is built on.

🥙Hygiene

Hours since your last food or water. Past ~24h the sesh gets harder to hold; by ~36h, much harder. Kebabs and waters reset it.

👥The boys (8)

Bucko + 7. They can chunder, get picked up, get thrown out, or vanish to a taxi. Drop to 2 standing and the night collapses.

05 Stacking the gear

Combinations matter. Some snap the night into balance; some file a formal objection with your heart.

Racks + K — the tandem

Coke onto ket calibrates the whole night. Untouchable, no seat for you. Big legend.

K + Pinger — the good roll

Ket after a pinger (or vice-versa) is the fabled roll. Glorious.

Racks + Pinger — bad stack

Coke on a pinger (either order) is dangerous — spikes heat and buzz, small cardiac chance.

Racks × 3+ — cardiac streak

Hammering coke over and over in a short window keeps a tally your heart eventually invoices. Grey, sat down, possibly an ambulance.

06 The crawl — venues & forks

You don't pick venues from a menu — the night fans out. Every so often a "where to, boys?" fork offers 3 venues from the current time-tier (± one), plus a kebab run when you're hot, plus "stick it out here." Doors get warier as your notoriety climbs; camp one spot too long and it goes cold; a lively, varied crawl builds momentum.

🎤 Rooms with their own rules

Connies sobriety-tests you on the door if you turn up anywhere near the top of the zone — follow the finger, send your steadiest in first, or take a water and come back in ten. Galaxy Lounge’s karaoke hostesses keep topping everyone up, so the tab climbs every tick you stay. The Reprobate is the only rooftop left where you can actually smoke. Storeys is four floors of nineteen-year-olds, and you are unmistakably the oldest men in the building.

🔒 The lock-in — a rare alternative to moving on

Late in the night (post ~1am), if you're dialled into the zone with real momentum — and it helps if Jez knows the publican — a fork can offer a fourth option: sweet-talk a lock-in. Low chance, earned by charm. Take it and you don't move on: you hold the pub past close as a permanent after-hours space. No more forks, no bouncers — but heat now means the risk of getting SPRUNG by a police lap. After ~90 minutes it bridges out to Leroy's kick-ons, making it an early on-ramp to the afters (degen carries over). Can happen at any pub; it just rarely does.

07 Kick-ons — pushing into the days

Make it to 6am sunrise and you can call it a perfect night — or KICK ON at Leroy's flat, where each “day” is a genuine 24 hours rolling over at dawn. From here you're chasing a degen score instead of legend, and every full day you push adds a bigger, dumber title.

At the flat the vibe shifts to house-party mode: goon o'clock on the Hills Hoist, neighbours instead of bouncers, Uber Eats instead of kebabs. Keep enough boys standing and enough buzz alive to "push into day N". The card bill hides; degen takes over as the headline number.

The wall — sleep comes for you

The further you get from sleep, the harder everything is to hold: buzz slides faster each day, and from day 2 a lucidity minigame (like the door quiz) starts firing — more often and harder the deeper you push, with shadow-people versions once they arrive. Answer well and you claw back; from day 4 a failure ends the run as THE WALL. You can outrun sleep for a while. Not forever.

08 The secret: the North Perth bowlo

At the very first fork only, one option is North Perth Bowling Club — presented like any normal venue, the only tell being that it's an odd call. It's a genuine, leave-able venue: most players have a couple and launch straight back into Northbridge. But stick it out past its midnight close and the whole game pivots.

  1. ~8:20pm — Arrive. A normal suburban lawn-bowls club — carpet bowls, a meat raffle, fluorescent light. Drink, then rejoin the crawl whenever you like.
  2. Midnight — last drinks. The club shuts like the suburban club it is, and you get a straight three-way choice: back into town for Connies, back into town for The Bird, or stay for the lock-in. Two of those are just a normal night. One isn't.
  3. Midnight → 2am — the lock-in. Doors locked from the inside, staff become guests, disco set out of the cupboard. Still just a very good private party at this stage.
  4. 2am — declare sovereignty. The point of no return. Your headline metric becomes the Movement (allegiance), with degen ticking underneath.
  5. The milestones — a three-day session. Ten beats fire across three days at kick-on speed, while you manage counterinsurgency, unavoidable derails, and hygiene. The local beats come first, then the world joins, then the last holdouts.
  6. Declare it. After the ratification and a full three days, you get to declare the global Doof Republic — the win. The whole takeover runs about six minutes of real time.

10 How you're scored

The end screen ranks the run. Legend drives the night rank; degen drives the kick-on rank; the bowlo run has its own Movement reach.

⭐Night — Legend rank

Built from total legend, boys standing, peak buzz, and the card bill. From keen amateur up to certified folk hero.

🔥Kick-ons — Degen rank

Merely Keen → Loose Unit → Certified Degen → Feral → Ungovernable, by degen score.

🌍Bowlo — Movement reach

Scaled by allegiance, all the way to Founder of the Doof Republic.
