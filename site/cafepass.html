Member experience

The usual, remembered. One tap to order, one code to collect.

The app opens to the member's saved order and nearest partner cafe. Sending the order ahead is optional — the daily QR works either way.

Preprogrammed usual order
Saved drink, milk, and extras load on open. Chip-based editing — two taps to switch to an oat latte, not a menu tree.
Order-ahead, optional
Submit sends the order to the cafe tablet queue with an order number. Members who'd rather just walk in still can — the QR is independent of ordering.
Single-scan daily credential
One QR per member per day, minted at 4am, burned on scan. Rotating signed tokens — screenshots don't work twice. 1 scan / 24h
