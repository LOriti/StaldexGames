Venue fanning

How the crawl fans out, and the numbers behind each pub — for tuning. The night runs through four time-tiers; you're never on rails, but each fork keeps you near the hour.

The fork rule. Each “where to, boys?” offers 3 venues from the current tier ± one (open ones only), plus a Kebab run when heat is high, plus “stick it out here.” Doors get warier as your notoriety climbs, and the rooftop stops letting you in once you're too marked. Camp a venue too long and it goes cold; a lively, varied crawl builds momentum.
Per venue: P = drink-price multiplier · L = legend rate · H = heat rate. Door = bouncer.
